Red-team round 5 — ATTEMPTS
===========================
Target: the try/finally of format 3, step 3 (verified/spec/format/
23-format3-try.md). TRY is a flagged block call; a pending unwinding is the
two token words in the finally code's slots [value, A, B, reason] (A =
frame << 16 | height, B = bci * 4 + kind; kind 0 normal, 1 exception, 2
non-local return, 3 non-local branch). No finally records.

Builds: build/host (64-bit release, verified core), build/asan
(-fsanitize=address,undefined). Each case: --untrusted (install-time
verifier) on both builds; trusted on the checked loop
(TOIT_TRUSTED_CHECKED_LOOP=1) on both builds.

One line per attempt: id | idea | expected | observed. Per-case output is
in repro/T-results.txt (battery-T.sh).

VERDICT: no finding. Every ill-typed case is rejected with a named rule
(UNWIND without Token kinds, a copy/store of a token, a token consumed as a
value, incompatible kinds at a merge, TRY on a non-block or a foreign
block); the well-typed cases print the right output; nothing crashes or
trips a sanitizer on either loop.
